# NaturAI-No_ARAS

NaturAI is an AI Botanist Chatbot that helps you grow your plants in the most easiest, smartest and rewarding way. NaturAI also can help you find plants that match your space, goals, and resources. It generates personalized care schedules, provides expert advice on plant health, and tracks progress with automated reminders and much more, essentially providing the user with an immersive personalized experience. Its designed for beginners, home gardeners, and even farmers.
